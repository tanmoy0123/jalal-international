About Jalal International Overseas

M/S Jalal International Overseas established in 1998 is a trusted name in the field of Manpower exporter organization in Bangladesh. Since its establishment, M/S Jalal International Overseas has maintained a leadership position through the hard work put in by its management. Now, we are one of the few professionally managed recruiting agencies in Bangladesh, approved by Government and holding Recruiting License No-612 to recruit and mobilize all categories of manpower from Bangladesh to overseas markets. see more
